# Content Skills: Multi-Platform Content Factory

A comprehensive agent skill for ideating, scripting, designing, and repurposing content across short-form video, carousels, and written posts.

## 1. Content Formats & Archetypes

### A. The 60-Second Video Reel Script
- **Hook (0-3s):** Spoken + kinetic headline punch.
- **Beat 1 (3-12s):** The Core Problem (Pain point magnification).
- **Beat 2 (12-30s):** The Solution (Step-by-step breakdown with B-roll cues).
- **Beat 3 (30-45s):** The Pro Secret (The unexpected insight).
- **Beat 4 (45-60s):** Call to Action (Comment trigger or link in bio).

### B. The 10-Slide Educational Carousel
- **Slide 1:** Hook / Magazine Cover (Visual curiosity trigger + clear promise).
- **Slide 2:** The "Why you are struggling" setup.
- **Slides 3–7:** 1 actionable tip/insight per slide with bold headers & clean typography.
- **Slide 8:** The Before vs. After transformation matrix.
- **Slide 9:** Summary / Cheat Sheet (encourages saves).
- **Slide 10:** CTA Slide ("Save this guide for your next project").

### C. The 1-to-5 Repurposing Engine
Take 1 core concept, case study, or tutorial and automatically produce:
1. **1x Short Reel** (30-second fast-cut transformation)
2. **1x Educational Carousel** (10-slide visual breakdown)
3. **1x Story Sequence** (3-part interactive poll & behind-the-scenes)
4. **1x Long-form Post / Caption** (Keyword-rich SEO caption with niche tags)
5. **1x Automated DM Lead Magnet** (PDF checklist or workflow resource prompt)

## 2. Audience Retention Checklist

Before any content is finalized, verify:
- [ ] First line hooks within 125 characters (visible before "...more" button on mobile).
- [ ] Contains zero robotic AI tells (banned words: "delve", "game-changer", "unleash", "tapestry").
- [ ] Natural conversational cadence in authentic creator voice (clear, engaging, direct).
- [ ] High save/share value (solves a specific audience bottleneck or reveals an efficient workflow).
- [ ] Maximum 5 relevant niche hashtags (e.g. `#VideoEditing`, `#ContentCreator`, `#ShortsGrowth`, `#MotionGraphics`, `#VideoProduction`).
